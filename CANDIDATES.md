# Candidate Jokes — The One-Line Cat Joke That Lands

Competition: u-aldy998-the-one-line-cat-joke-that-lands-a7b18262
Brief: one joke about cats, a single line, under 200 characters. Judge (GLM-5.3-flash,
stand-up comic persona) picks the funnier of two jokes, weighing in order:
1. one-line cat joke with a setup and a turn that lands
2. laughs out loud on first read, not just a clever smile
3. surprise / misdirection / fresh angle, not a heard-it-before pun
4. few words, no padding, no wind-up, no explanation after the punchline
5. feels retellable to a friend the same day

Rules I applied: no tired puns (purr-fect, meow, cat-astrophe, paws), no
"a cat walks into a bar", original wording throughout, one line only.

## Reasoning

The strongest three were #3, #12, and #16.

- #3 ("haunted house") has a great turn — the cat staring at a blank wall
  reframes the human as a tenant in the cat's haunted house. Vivid and fresh.
  Slightly weaker on brevity: "and honestly, at this point" is wind-up.
- #16 (vet / underfed) is the tightest and most retellable, a classic three-beat
  reversal. But the "the cat talks back" shape is a familiar joke format, so it
  scores lower on surprise/freshness — the judge explicitly penalizes jokes
  it has heard many times before.
- #12 inverts the most famous cat fact there is (nine lives) instead of
  repeating it: one real life plus eight fake death performances. The turn lands
  in the second sentence, the punchline ("dying for attention") is a fresh,
  specific observation about dramatic cats, and the whole thing reads like
  something a person would actually say to a friend the same day. No pun, no
  wind-up, no explanation after the punchline.

## Chosen entry

> My cat doesn't have nine lives. He has one life and eight elaborate ways of making me think he's dying for attention.
